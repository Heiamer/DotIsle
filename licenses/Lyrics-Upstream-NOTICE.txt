DotIsle ports the automatic online source order, source HTTP contracts, matching,
search variants, secondary alignment, preview alignment and online parsers to Go/Rust.
The upstream C++ sources are not required at runtime. Local files and the existing
DotIsle media/display adapters keep their own implementations. Product User-Agent
identifies DotIsle. No forwarding service or Java runtime is used.
